# Loughran-McDonald sentiment word lists

Positive, negative and uncertainty (hedging) word lists from the Loughran-McDonald Master
Dictionary (Tim Loughran and Bill McDonald, University of Notre Dame, https://sraf.nd.edu/).
Extracted from the copy distributed in the `pysentiment2` Python package (v0.1.1).
Free for academic and personal use; commercial use requires a license from the authors.
Used here for a single-user, non-commercial research tool.
